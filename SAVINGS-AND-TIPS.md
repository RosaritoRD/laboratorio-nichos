# 💸 Savings and tips

## Spend less

1. **The biggest saving is not launching a bad niche.** Building a channel costs weeks, plus money on voice, AI and editing. The validator tells you **DON'T LAUNCH** before you spend anything.
2. **Zero dependencies and zero APIs.** The inbox uses only Node's standard library. No paid services and nothing to update every month.
3. **Use Google login for free.** `oauth2-proxy` handles security without you having to code users or passwords.
4. **Capture on your phone, work later.** Ideas don't get lost, and the coding assistant does the heavy lifting when you sit down.

## Do it better

1. **Don't lower the bar.** If winning channels are missing, find more or drill deeper into the topic. Skipping a filter is the #1 reason channels never get monetized.
2. **Copy the format from English channels and the language from the Spanish competition.** English-language YouTube is months ahead.
3. **Distrust any list of "niches that pay".** The idea bank ships candidates without numbers on purpose; the numbers come from your own validation.
4. **One niche, one folder.** With `nuevo-nicho.sh` each channel keeps its own memory and notes, and the assistant doesn't confuse styles.
5. **Ideas to extend it:** pull channel data automatically from the YouTube API, track month-over-month changes, or alert when a niche's verdict changes.
